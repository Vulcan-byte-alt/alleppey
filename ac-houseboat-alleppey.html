04 · AC houseboat tips

Ask before you pay

Four questions about AC are worth asking before you book an AC houseboat.

  1. Which rooms? Bedroom only, or living and dining too?
  2. Which hours? When does the AC run?
  3. Day cruise AC? Is it available, and at what cost?
  4. Full-time upgrade? Included, or extra on this boat?
On-board tips
  • Close doors and windows of the AC room.
  • Bring a light layer for cool AC rooms.
  • Spend the evening on the open deck for the breeze and sunset.

FAQ

Quick answers

Do all houseboats in Alleppey have AC?

No. Deluxe boats have bedroom AC at night only, from 9 PM to 6 AM. Premium has night AC with full-time AC as an upgrade.

Luxury runs full AC 24 hours across bedrooms, living and dining areas.

What are the AC timings on a houseboat?

Deluxe AC runs from 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM in the bedroom. During the day you rely on natural ventilation and the breeze off the water.

Luxury AC runs around the clock.

Is AC available on a day cruise?

Our standard day cruise is non-AC. Daytime AC can be added at extra cost. Ask when you enquire so we can quote it for your boat.

How much more does an AC houseboat cost?

Deluxe has night AC. Premium has night AC with full AC as an upgrade.

Luxury has full AC included. Pricing is per boat and varies by season, so contact us for a quote.

Which AC houseboat is best in summer?

For April and May we recommend Luxury, or Premium with the full-time AC upgrade. Both keep living areas cool through the afternoon.
