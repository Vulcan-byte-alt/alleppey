Best Time to Visit Alleppey for a Houseboat Experience

May 18, 2026 · 6 min read · By Alleppey Homes

The best time to visit Alleppey for a houseboat depends on weather, budget and how busy you like the water. Here is the short, honest version.

01 · October to March, the peak season

Clear skies, calm water

October to March is the peak season, and the finest window for a houseboat in Alleppey. Skies are clear, the air is cool and the channels are still.

Clear and calm

The northeast monsoon ends by mid-October, leaving dry air and easy, unhurried cruising.

24 to 32°C days

Warm enough for the open deck and never harsh, so long afternoons outside are easy.

Birds and nets

Kingfishers, cormorants and brahminy kites, with Chinese fishing nets silhouetted at sunset.

Practical tip

Book 4 to 6 weeks ahead for weekends and 2 to 3 weeks ahead for weekdays to get your preferred tier: Deluxe, Premium or Luxury.

02 · Christmas and New Year

Lights on the lake

Christmas and New Year, 23 December to 2 January, is the most sought-after window of the year. It is festive and lovely, so plan early and budget for it.

1.5 to 2×

Standard rate

Houseboat rates typically run 1.5 to 2 times the usual price.

3 to 6 months

Book ahead

Reputable operators fill up this far in advance for this window.

Direct

Skip the markup

Booking directly, not via an OTA, avoids platform markup on already-high prices.

03 · Monsoon, June to September

When the rains arrive

The monsoon runs from June to September. The southwest rains turn the paddies a deep green and soften the waterways. Locals call it karkidakam, a season of plenty and quiet.

What changes

  • Shorter cruising hours in heavy rain
  • Boats keep to the main channels, not narrow village canals

What you gain

  • Noticeably lower rates
  • Thinner crowds and a personal feel
Before you book

Visiting between June and September? Confirm cruising hours with us first. We give an honest picture of what the weather is likely to allow on your dates.

04 · April and May, summer

Long days on the water

April and May are the hottest months, with afternoons sometimes above 35°C. They are also good value.

  • Open-deck time is best early morning and evening
  • Air-conditioned cabins keep the heat of the day away
  • Moderate rates, and school-holiday crowds have not yet arrived
  • May sunsets on the water are some of the reddest of the year
