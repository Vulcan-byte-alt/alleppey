FAQ

Quick answers

Which houseboat is best for a family in Alleppey?

A two-bedroom boat suits a family of four, in Deluxe, Premium or Luxury, with meals.

Pricing varies by season, so contact us for a quote. Bigger boats are available on request.

Is a houseboat cruise safe for children?

Yes. The boats are wide, stable platforms with life jackets for everyone and a licensed crew.

Swimming is not permitted. Keep young children supervised on the open deck.

What do children pay on a houseboat?

Children below 5 years are free and ages 5 to 12 pay 50%. Ask us about extra guests above the listed number.

Is food on board suitable for kids and vegetarians?

Yes. Meals are cooked fresh on board, and vegetarian, vegan and Jain meals can be arranged with 24 hours of notice.

Tell us about allergies when you book.

Is a day cruise or overnight better with children?

A 5-hour day cruise is easy with young children and returns by 5 PM.

Overnight suits older children, with a sunset, dinner on deck and morning birdsong.

Do you offer a family package?

Yes. Family Explorer is 4 days and 3 nights for 4 people. Contact us for pricing.

It includes 2 homestay nights, a day premium houseboat cruise, a village tour, a toddy shop visit, fishing, bird watching and traditional meals.
