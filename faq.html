Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about staying at Alleppey Homes

What is included in the homestay experience? ▼
Our homestay includes authentic Kerala accommodation (AC or Non-AC options), traditional home-cooked meals, WiFi, free parking, and personalized hospitality. We help you experience the real Alappuzha backwaters with local insights and cultural experiences.
Can I book a houseboat through you? ▼
Yes! We arrange the best traditional houseboat cruises in Alleppey. Our packages include professional crew, fresh local food, and unforgettable sunset experiences on the Alappuzha backwaters. We offer half-day, full-day, and overnight cruises.
What's the best time to visit Alleppey? ▼
October to March offers pleasant weather (20-30°C) ideal for sightseeing and houseboat cruises. The monsoon season (June-September) offers unique lush green beauty and lower prices, though rain is frequent. April-May can be hot but less crowded.
How do I reach your homestay? ▼
We're located in South Aryad, Alleppey. From Cochin Airport (85km): 2-hour taxi/Uber. From Alappuzha Railway Station (6km): 15-minute ride. We can arrange pickup/drop for a nominal fee. Address: Darul Hamdu, Avulukunnu P.O., South Aryad, Alleppey 688006.
What is your cancellation policy? ▼
Homestay: Free cancellation 7+ days before check-in. 50% refund if cancelled within 7 days. Houseboat: Free cancellation 14+ days before cruise date. Full amount forfeited if cancelled within 14 days. No-shows forfeit entire booking.
What are check-in/check-out times? ▼
Check-in: 2:00 PM | Check-out: 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out subject to availability (may incur additional charges). Please inform us in advance if you need flexible timings.
Do you provide meals? ▼
Yes! We serve authentic Kerala home-cooked meals. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner available. We can accommodate dietary restrictions (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free) with advance notice. All meals feature fresh local ingredients and traditional recipes.
Is WiFi available? ▼
Yes, free high-speed WiFi is available throughout the homestay. Perfect for digital nomads or staying connected with family!
Are children welcome? ▼
Absolutely! We're a family-friendly homestay. Children love exploring the backwaters, feeding fish, and experiencing village life. We can arrange child-friendly activities and provide age-appropriate meals.
What activities can I do in Alleppey? ▼
Houseboat cruises, canoe rides, village tours, bird watching, fishing, visiting toddy shops, cycling through paddy fields, sunset viewing, cultural performances, visiting local temples, Ayurvedic massages, and exploring Alleppey beach.
How much does a houseboat cruise cost? ▼
Prices vary by season and houseboat category. Budget options start from ₹8,000-10,000 for full-day cruises. Premium houseboats with AC range from ₹12,000-20,000. Contact us for current rates and availability.
Do you arrange airport transfers? ▼
Yes, we can arrange comfortable airport/railway station transfers for a reasonable fee. Just let us know your arrival details in advance and we'll have transport ready for you.
