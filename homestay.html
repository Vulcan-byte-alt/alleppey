About our homestay

Coconut palms and a quiet lane

A family home in the backwater village of South Aryad, 10 to 15 minutes from Alleppey town.

  • A short auto or cab ride from the main boat jetty at Finishing Point
  • Walled garden of coconut palms, banana trees and flowers, with off-street parking
  • Fresh breakfast daily: puttu with kadala curry or appam with vegetable stew, plus chai and filter coffee
More about the house and the food

A quiet corner of the garden is where guests often take breakfast. Lanes around the house lead into the village: canals, paddy fields and local temples, all within walking distance.

Lunch and dinner bring rice, sambar, vegetable dishes and a fish or chicken curry, made with backwater fish when available.

Homestay questions

Before you pack

What makes your homestay in Alleppey authentic?

Our homestay is our family home in Alleppey, and you stay with us as our guests. We serve home-cooked traditional meals, share our culture and customs, and give personal care that a hotel cannot. You can join daily family life, learn traditional cooking and hear local stories.

What meals are included when booking?

A traditional Kerala breakfast comes with every stay. Our AC room package includes all three meals, and the traditional room includes breakfast, with lunch and dinner on request. We cater to dietary needs and allergies with advance notice.

Is your family homestay suitable for children?

Yes. We are family-run and love welcoming families. There is a safe garden for children to play in, and we provide child-friendly meals. Children enjoy the tropical garden, helping in the kitchen and learning about Kerala culture.

Can you arrange houseboat trips from the homestay?

Yes. We help you book day cruises and overnight houseboat stays in Alleppey through trusted local operators at fair prices, so you can combine the village stay with time on the water.

What is the difference between AC and non-AC rooms?

The non-AC room is Rs 1,500 per night with ceiling fans, natural ventilation and breakfast. The AC room is Rs 2,500 per night with air conditioning, all meals and extras like cultural guidance and local tour arrangements. Both give the same warm hospitality.

How far is the homestay from Alleppey town?

We are in the backwater village area of South Aryad, about 10 to 15 minutes from the town centre and a short auto or cab ride from the main boat jetty at Finishing Point.
