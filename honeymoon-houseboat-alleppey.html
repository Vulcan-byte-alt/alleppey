03 · A simple honeymoon plan

Three slow days

A simple honeymoon plan in Alleppey gives you three days and one night on the water.

Day 1

Arrive. Settle into our homestay and enjoy a home-cooked dinner.

Day 2

Cruise. Board at noon, dine on deck and sleep on the water.

Day 3

Sunrise. Breakfast on board, check-out at 9 AM.

FAQ

Quick answers

Which houseboat is best for a honeymoon in Alleppey?

A Luxury overnight boat is our top pick, with full AC, glass-covered views and an upper deck.

Premium boats are a lovely alternative. Contact us for pricing.

What does the honeymoon special include?

Romantic decoration, flower petals, a candle-light dinner and a complimentary cake for couples.

Ask us about the set-up for a day cruise or an overnight stay.

Is a day cruise or overnight stay better for honeymooners?

Overnight is highly recommended: dinner on deck, a quiet night on the water and a sunrise over the lake.

A day cruise suits short trips.

Do you offer a honeymoon package with a homestay?

Yes. Our Honeymoon Special is 3 days and 2 nights for couples. Contact us for pricing.

It includes an AC homestay night, a day luxury houseboat cruise, candle-light dinner, flower decoration, all meals, a sunset cruise and a photography session.

Will we have the houseboat to ourselves?

Yes. Every houseboat booking is private, with your own bedroom, deck and crew.

When is the best time for a honeymoon cruise in Alleppey?

October to March has the most pleasant weather. The monsoon is lush and quieter, and summer is best on an AC boat.

Book early for December and January.
