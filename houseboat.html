05 · Your houseboat cruise

Silence, dinner and stars

Your Alleppey houseboat cruise leaves the Finishing Point, crosses Vembanad Lake and slips into quiet canals, about 25 to 35 km in all.

Lake, then canals

Lake first, then canals past paddy fields, palms and villages.

A boat that is yours

A hand-built kettuvallam, privately yours for the whole booking.

Anchored for the dark

Dinner on deck, birdsong and a sky full of stars.

Local tip

Kerala rules anchor every houseboat from about 5:30 PM to 7:00 AM. It protects fishermen, and gives you a quiet night.

07 · Houseboat safety and responsible tourism

Safe on the water

Kerala inspects houseboats on Vembanad Lake closely, and we work only with licensed, compliant operators.

Gear on board

Life jackets for all, fire extinguishers, first-aid kit.

A crew of three

Captain, chef and engine driver on every boat.

Gentle on the lake

Bio-toilets and Vembanad Lake pollution norms.

Enjoy it from deck

Not permitted: depth, boat traffic and currents.

Clean and fresh

Rooms and kitchens cleaned before each booking.

Easy with children

Wide, stable boats for children and non-swimmers.

08 · How to reach Alleppey

Finding the jetty

To reach your Alleppey houseboat, head for the Finishing Point, the main check-in. The others are Punnamada, Mullakkal, Thathampally and Nedumudy.

How far it is

  • Cochin airport: about 100 km
  • Trivandrum airport: about 160 km
  • Ernakulam: about 65 km
  • Station and bus stand: about 2 km

Before you arrive

  • Check-in 12:00 PM
  • Check-out 9:00 AM or 5:00 PM (day)
  • 50% deposit, balance on check-in
  • Government taxes apply

Houseboat FAQ

Your questions, answered

What types of houseboats can I book in Alleppey?

We arrange three tiers of traditional kettuvallam houseboat: Deluxe (night AC), Premium (glass-covered with panoramic views) and Luxury (full AC and premium amenities).

Every boat has a licensed crew, a cook and fresh meals on board. See the luxury and AC houseboat guides.

What is the price of a one-day houseboat trip in Alleppey?

Pricing varies by season, boat and group size, so we quote each cruise for your dates. A 5-hour day cruise includes a welcome drink, lunch and evening tea with snacks.

Children below 5 cruise free. Contact us for pricing, or read our houseboat price guide.

Are overnight houseboat stays available in Alappuzha?

Yes. Overnight stays run from 12 PM to 9 AM the next day with all meals included. Pricing is per boat and varies by boat category and season, so contact us for a quote.

The boat is anchored for the night, so you get dinner on deck, a quiet sleep on the water and a sunrise over the lake.

What is the best time to visit Alleppey for a houseboat cruise?

October to March is the most pleasant. The monsoon (June to September) is lush with limited cruising, and April to May is warm, so an AC boat is a good idea.

December to January is peak season and needs early booking. Read our best time to visit guide.

Is food included in the houseboat price?

Yes. Day cruises include a welcome drink, lunch, evening tea and snacks. Overnight stays include breakfast, lunch, dinner, tea or coffee, snacks and mineral water.

Vegetarian, vegan and Jain meals can be arranged if you tell us at least 24 hours ahead.

How do I book a houseboat in Alleppey?

Send us your dates, group size and preferred tier through the contact page or WhatsApp. We confirm availability and a clear price, and you pay a 50% advance by UPI, bank transfer or cash. The balance is paid on check-in.

What are the AC hours on a houseboat?

Deluxe boats have AC in the bedroom from 9 PM to 6 AM. Premium boats have night AC with full-time AC as an extra-cost upgrade. Luxury boats run full AC for 24 hours across the bedrooms, living and dining areas.

More in our AC houseboat guide.

Which is better, a day cruise or an overnight stay?

A day cruise suits short itineraries, young children or first-timers who are unsure about sleeping on water. An overnight stay is the full experience, with an evening and a sunrise on the lake, and is highly recommended for couples.

Many guests pair it with a night at our Alleppey homestay. See day cruise vs overnight.

Are vegetarian or Jain meals available on the houseboat?

Yes. Pure vegetarian, Jain and custom meals can be arranged when you book. Please tell us about dietary needs or allergies at least 24 hours before your cruise.

What route does the houseboat take?

Cruises start at the Alleppey Finishing Point, head into Vembanad Lake, then thread through narrow canals past paddy fields, coconut groves and villages before returning to the same jetty.

Depending on the package this covers roughly 25 to 35 km of waterways.

Are houseboats anchored at night?

Yes. Kerala regulations require houseboats to anchor from around 5:30 PM until 7:00 AM, which protects local fishermen who lay nets at night. Most guests find the quiet evening anchored on the water the highlight of the trip.

How early should I book for Christmas or New Year?

December 20 to January 5 is the busiest period. Good boats often sell out three to six months ahead, and pricing is higher than usual.

Contact us by September to hold a boat with a 50% advance.

Are private houseboats available for couples and families?

Yes. Every booking is a fully private boat. Nobody else shares your bedrooms, living area, dining deck or crew. See our honeymoon and family houseboat guides.
