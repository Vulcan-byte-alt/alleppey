Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and enjoying your houseboat experience in Alleppey

Through our partners in Alleppey, we can arrange three main types of houseboats: Deluxe (with night AC), Premium (glass-covered with panoramic views), and Luxury (full AC and premium amenities). All offer an authentic Kettuvallam experience.

Each houseboat type is fully equipped with professional crew, fresh meals prepared onboard, and all necessary safety equipment for a comfortable and memorable experience.

A 5-hour day cruise in Alleppey starts at Rs. 7,500 for 2 people, including lunch and snacks. Prices vary based on the number of guests. Please see our rates table for more details.

Additional charges apply for AC (if required during day time), special decorations, and extra services. Children below 5 years cruise for free, and we offer group discounts for larger parties.

Yes, we arrange overnight houseboat stays in Alappuzha. The cruise typically runs from 12 PM to 9 AM the next day, with the boat anchored safely at night. All meals are included.

During overnight stays, houseboats are anchored in safe, designated areas from 6 PM to 7 AM. You can enjoy dinner under the stars, peaceful sleep on the water, and wake up to a stunning sunrise over the backwaters.

The best time for houseboat cruises in Alleppey is from October to March when the weather is pleasant. Monsoon season (June-September) offers lush green scenery but limited cruising. Summer months (April-May) are warm but manageable with AC houseboats.

Peak season (December-January) requires advance booking due to high demand. Off-season offers better rates and a more peaceful experience with fewer crowds.

Yes, all our houseboat packages include fresh meals prepared onboard. Day cruises include welcome drink, lunch, evening tea and snacks. Overnight cruises include breakfast, lunch, dinner, tea/coffee, snacks and mineral water.

Our experienced cooks prepare authentic Kerala cuisine using fresh local ingredients. Special dietary requirements (vegetarian, vegan, allergies) can be accommodated with advance notice.

You can book through our website contact form, WhatsApp (+91-9947-048197), or phone (+91-9946-021397). We require 50% advance payment to confirm booking, with balance payable on check-in.

We accept UPI, bank transfer, and cash payments. Booking confirmation includes detailed itinerary, check-in instructions, and contact information for the day of your cruise.
