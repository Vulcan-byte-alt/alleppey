Your Alleppey Houseboat Experience

A private journey through Kerala's backwaters — from the first bend of the canal to stars over still water.

The Route

25–35 km Through Living Kerala

Every cruise departs from the Alleppey Finishing Point and heads south into Vembanad Lake — Kerala's largest lake. Narrow canals then thread through paddy fields, toddy palms, and village life unchanged for centuries, before the route circles back to the same jetty.

Your Boat

Entirely Yours. No Strangers.

We work exclusively with owner-operated Kettuvallam boats — hand-built from bamboo, coir rope, and jack wood, with no nails. Every boat has an experienced captain and a personal cook preparing fresh Kerala meals on board. The entire vessel is privately yours for the duration.

The Night

Silence. Dinner. Stars.

The boat anchors between 5:30 PM and 7:00 AM — a Kerala government rule protecting local fishermen. In practice: dinner on an open deck as the sun drops over the water, complete silence except for birds, and a sky full of stars you won't find in any hotel room.

Safety & Responsible Tourism

Kerala's High Court has directed strict inspections of houseboats on Vembanad Lake. We only work with licensed, compliant operators — here's what that means for you.

Safety Equipment

Life jackets provided for all passengers. Fire extinguishers, first-aid kit, and emergency flotation devices onboard. All vessels carry mandatory Kerala Tourism Dept. certification.

Licensed Crew

Every houseboat operates with a minimum 3-person licensed crew: a Captain, a Chef, and an Engine Driver — trained, certified, and present at all times during your cruise.

Eco Compliance

Our partner houseboats use bio-toilets and follow Vembanad Lake pollution norms. Houseboats are anchored by 5:30 PM to protect local fishermen — a government regulation we fully respect.

Swimming Advisory

Swimming in Vembanad Lake and the backwater canals is strictly not permitted — unpredictable depths, boat traffic, and underwater currents make it unsafe. Enjoy the water visually from the deck.

Hygiene Standards

All rooms, bathrooms, and kitchen areas are thoroughly cleaned and sanitised before each booking. Bed linens, towels, and utensils are freshly laundered. Mineral water provided throughout.

Family & Non-Swimmers

Houseboat cruises are completely safe for children, elderly guests, and non-swimmers. The boats are wide, stable platforms. Children below 5 cruise free; ages 5–12 get 50% discount.
