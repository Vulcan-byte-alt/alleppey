How to Reach Alleppey

Check-in Points

  • • Alleppey Finishing Point (Major check-in point) - Heart of Alleppey town
  • • Punnamada, Mullakkal, Thathampally, Nedumudy

Nearest Airports

  • • Cochin Airport to Alleppey - 100 Km
  • • Trivandrum Airport to Alleppey - 160 Km

Nearest Railway Stations

  • • Alleppey Railway Station to Finishing Point - 2 Km
  • • Ernakulam (Town/Junction) to Alleppey - 65 Km

Nearest Bus Stand

  • • Alleppey KSRTC Bus stand to Finishing Point - 2 Km

Important Information

Timing: Check-in 12:00 PM | Check-out 09:00 AM
Anchoring: Houseboats are anchored from 6 PM till 7 AM for night stay
Meals Included: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Tea/Coffee, Snacks & Mineral water
Children: Below 5 years complimentary
Payment: 50% deposit required, balance on check-in
Safety: All rooms thoroughly disinfected before each check-in
Taxes: Rates subject to India Government Taxes & Levies
