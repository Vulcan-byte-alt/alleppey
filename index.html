Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your Kerala backwaters experience

Our homestay includes authentic Kerala accommodation with either AC or Non-AC rooms based on your choice. You'll enjoy traditional home-cooked meals featuring local cuisine, complimentary WiFi, safe parking, and personalized hospitality from our family.

We also provide local guidance for attractions, help with transportation arrangements, and can connect you with authentic houseboat experiences through our trusted local operators.

Yes, we arrange traditional houseboat cruises through our local connections. These luxury houseboats come with professional crew, fresh seafood meals, and sunset experiences on Kerala's pristine backwaters.

Houseboat pricing varies based on duration, season, and specific requirements. Contact us for custom packages that combine homestay and houseboat experiences for the perfect Kerala getaway.

Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. However, we understand that travel plans can vary, so we offer flexible timing whenever possible to accommodate your schedule.

If you need early check-in or late check-out, please let us know in advance and we'll do our best to arrange it based on availability.

Yes, we can arrange pickup from airport or railway station. This service is included in our AC homestay package and available as an add-on for other packages.

We coordinate with reliable local drivers who know the area well and will ensure your safe and comfortable journey to our homestay. Advance booking is recommended.

We accept cash payments, UPI transfers, and bank transfers for your convenience. For advance bookings, we typically require a small deposit to confirm your reservation.

Payment can be made upon arrival or in advance. We'll provide our bank details and UPI information once you confirm your booking. No hidden charges - all costs are transparently communicated upfront.

The best time to visit Kerala is from October to March when the weather is pleasant and ideal for backwater cruises and outdoor activities. The monsoon season (June-September) offers a unique experience with lush green landscapes.

Summer months (April-May) can be warm, but our AC accommodations ensure comfort. Each season has its own charm - monsoons for romance, winter for activities, and summer for peaceful relaxation.
