01 · What makes a luxury houseboat

Glass, cool air, open deck

A luxury houseboat in Alleppey keeps the traditional kettuvallam shape and adds glass-covered living spaces, constant AC and a viewing deck.

03 · A day and night on a luxury houseboat

From noon till sunrise

Check-in on a luxury houseboat is at 12:00 PM at the Finishing Point, and check-out is at 9:00 AM.

The afternoon

Cruise Vembanad Lake and quiet canals with lunch on board.

The evening

The boat anchors for the night. Dinner on deck, cool AC bedroom.

The morning

Sunrise breakfast, then check-out at 9:00 AM.

Packing tips

Bring a camera for the upper deck, light clothing and a layer for the AC rooms.

Swimming in the lake and canals is not permitted, so enjoy the water from the deck.

FAQ

Quick answers

What makes a luxury houseboat different in Alleppey?

Luxury boats are glass-covered for panoramic views, run full AC for 24 hours, add an upper viewing deck and serve premium meals.

Deluxe boats are traditional open-deck kettuvallams with night AC only.

How much is a luxury houseboat in Alleppey?

Luxury pricing is per boat and varies by season, bedrooms and guest count, so we quote each stay for your dates.

Meals are included, and pricing is higher in December and January. Contact us for pricing.

Is a Premium houseboat good enough, or should I choose Luxury?

Premium is glass-covered with panoramic views and a full-AC upgrade.

Choose Luxury for continuous AC, the upper viewing deck and our most premium experience, such as a honeymoon.

Are luxury houseboats private?

Yes. Every booking is a fully private boat with its own captain, chef and engine driver. You never share it.

Can I combine a luxury houseboat with a homestay?

Yes. Many guests pair a night on a luxury boat with our family homestay in South Aryad.

Our Luxury Backwaters package covers 5 days and 4 nights for couples. Contact us for pricing.
