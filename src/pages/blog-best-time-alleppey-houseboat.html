Best Time to Visit Alleppey Boat House (Houseboat)

Updated November 17, 2026 · 6 min read · By Alleppey Homes

The best time to visit an Alleppey boat house (a houseboat, or kettuvallam) depends on weather, budget and how busy you like the water. The short answer: October to March for the best weather, and June to September for the lowest rates. Here is the honest version.

01 · October to March, the peak season

Clear skies, calm water

October to March is the peak season, and the finest window for a houseboat in Alleppey. Skies are clear, the air is cool and the channels are still.

Clear and calm

The northeast monsoon ends by mid-October, leaving dry air and easy, unhurried cruising.

24 to 32°C days

Warm enough for the open deck and never harsh, so long afternoons outside are easy.

Birds and nets

Kingfishers, cormorants and brahminy kites, with Chinese fishing nets silhouetted at sunset.

Practical tip

Book 4 to 6 weeks ahead for weekends and 2 to 3 weeks ahead for weekdays to get your preferred tier: Deluxe, Premium or Luxury.

02 · Christmas and New Year

Lights on the lake

03 · Monsoon, June to September

When the rains arrive

The monsoon runs from June to September. The southwest rains turn the paddies a deep green and soften the waterways. Locals call it karkidakam, a season of plenty and quiet.

What changes

  • Shorter cruising hours in heavy rain
  • Boats keep to the main channels, not narrow village canals

What you gain

  • Noticeably lower rates
  • Thinner crowds and a personal feel
Before you book

Visiting between June and September? Confirm cruising hours with us first. We give an honest picture of what the weather is likely to allow on your dates.

04 · April and May, summer

Long days on the water

April and May are the hottest months, with afternoons sometimes above 35°C. They are also good value.

  • Open-deck time is best early morning and evening
  • Air-conditioned cabins keep the heat of the day away
  • Moderate rates, and school-holiday crowds have not yet arrived
  • May sunsets on the water are some of the reddest of the year

06 · Quick answers

Alleppey boat house season, answered

What is the best time to visit an Alleppey boat house?

October to March. Skies are clear, days run about 24 to 32°C and the backwaters are calm.

What is the Alleppey boat house season?

The main season runs from October to March. The busiest window is 23 December to 2 January. June to September is the monsoon, with lower rates and shorter cruising hours.

Can I take an Alleppey boat house during the monsoon?

Yes. Boats run from June to September, but they keep to the main channels and cruising hours can be shorter in heavy rain. Rates are noticeably lower. Confirm cruising hours with us for your dates.

How early should I book an Alleppey houseboat?

Book 4 to 6 weeks ahead for weekends and 2 to 3 weeks ahead for weekdays in peak season. For Christmas and New Year, book 3 to 6 months ahead.

Is April or May a good time for a houseboat in Alleppey?

April and May are hot, with afternoons sometimes above 35°C, but they are good value. Air-conditioned cabins keep the heat away, and early mornings and evenings on the open deck are pleasant.
