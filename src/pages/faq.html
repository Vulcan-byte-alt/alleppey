Homestay

What is included in the homestay?

Your room (AC or non-AC), traditional home-cooked Kerala meals, free Wi-Fi, free parking and personal hospitality from our family. We also help with local guidance and transport.

How much does a homestay cost?

The non-AC room is Rs 1,500 per night and includes a traditional Kerala breakfast. The AC room is Rs 2,500 per night and includes all meals, cultural guidance and local tour arrangements.

Do you provide meals?

Yes. We serve authentic Kerala home-cooked breakfast, lunch and dinner. We can accommodate vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free needs with advance notice, and all meals use fresh local ingredients and traditional recipes.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes, free high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the homestay, handy for remote work or keeping in touch with family.

Is parking available?

Yes. We have free, secure off-street parking at the homestay.

Are children welcome?

Yes. We are a family-friendly homestay with a safe garden. Children love exploring the backwaters, feeding fish and seeing village life, and we can arrange child-friendly activities and age-appropriate meals.

Is the homestay a good choice for a honeymoon or couple?

Yes. Couples enjoy the quiet garden and private rooms, and we can add a houseboat cruise, candlelight dinner and flower decoration through our honeymoon package.

Houseboats

Can I book a houseboat through you?

Yes. We arrange traditional houseboat cruises in Alleppey with a professional crew, fresh local food and a sunset on the backwaters. We offer half-day, full-day and overnight cruises.

How much does a houseboat cruise cost?

Houseboat prices vary by season, boat category and cruise length, so we quote each trip individually. Contact us for current rates and availability.

Getting here & planning

What is the best time to visit Alleppey?

October to March has pleasant weather of about 20 to 30 degrees, ideal for sightseeing and houseboat cruises. The monsoon from June to September is lush and quieter with lower prices, though rain is frequent. April and May are hot but less crowded.

How do I reach your homestay?

We are in South Aryad, Alleppey. From Cochin Airport, about 85 km, it is roughly a 2 hour taxi ride. From Alappuzha Railway Station, about 6 km, it is a 15 minute ride. Our address is Darul Hamdu, Avulukunnu P.O., South Aryad, Alleppey 688006.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may incur additional charges, so please tell us in advance.

What can we do in Alleppey?

Houseboat cruises, canoe rides, village tours, bird watching, fishing, toddy shop visits, cycling through paddy fields, sunset viewing, cultural performances, local temples, Ayurvedic massages and Alleppey beach.

Booking & payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, UPI and bank transfers. For advance bookings we usually ask for a small deposit to confirm your reservation, and we tell you every cost upfront.

How do I book?

Call or WhatsApp us, or send an enquiry through the contact page with your dates and group size. We reply personally with availability and a clear price.
