03 · Why houseboat prices differ

What moves the price

Five things change the cost of a houseboat in Alleppey.

The boat itself

Deluxe, Premium and Luxury differ in comfort, views and AC.

Rooms and people

One bedroom suits two guests, two bedrooms suit four.

Day or night

Overnight adds dinner, breakfast and a night aboard.

The calendar

December and January cost most. The monsoon is usually gentler on the budget.

Cool hours

Daytime AC, or full-time AC on Premium, costs extra.

Best value

A weekday day cruise outside the peak is the lightest way in. Among overnight boats, a Deluxe one-bedroom is the most modest.

04 · Houseboat booking for Christmas and New Year

Christmas, booked early

December 20 to January 5 is the busiest time for houseboat booking in Alleppey.

Book by September

Good boats often sell out three to six months ahead, and pricing is higher than usual. We hold a boat with a 50% advance.

Ways to get more for your budget
  • Travel outside December and January.
  • Pick a day cruise if you do not need to sleep aboard.
  • Choose Deluxe in cooler months and Luxury for April and May.
  • Fill the boat: two bedrooms for four guests costs less per head.
  • Ask for a confirmed quote that lists meals, taxes and AC hours.

Every quote lists what is included. You pay only a 50% advance to confirm.

FAQ

Quick answers

How much does a houseboat cost in Alleppey?

Houseboat prices in Alleppey vary by season, boat category, air conditioning and group size, so we quote each booking for your dates.

Overnight pricing is per boat and includes meals. Contact us for a quote.

Is the houseboat price per person or per boat?

Overnight pricing is per boat, not per person, and covers the guest count listed. Day cruise pricing is set by group size. Ask us about extra guests.

What is included in the houseboat price?

Day cruises include a welcome drink, lunch and evening tea with snacks. Overnight stays add dinner, breakfast, tea or coffee and mineral water.

A licensed crew of captain, chef and engine driver is part of every booking.

Do children pay for houseboat cruises?

Children below 5 years are free and ages 5 to 12 get a 50% discount. Ask us about extra guests above the listed number.

Why do houseboat prices change?

Season, boat category, air conditioning, group size and demand all matter. December and January cost the most.

Around Christmas and New Year, pricing is higher than usual.

How do I pay for a houseboat booking?

Pay a 50% advance by UPI, bank transfer or cash to confirm. The balance is due on check-in.

Government taxes apply.
