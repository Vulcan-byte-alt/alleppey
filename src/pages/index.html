Our story

Mornings without a hurry

We are a family in South Aryad, a few minutes from the Alappuzha backwaters. We keep things simple: clean rooms, honest prices and food from our own kitchen.

Wake to misty water and coconut palms, share stories over a home-cooked breakfast, and let us point you to the canals, villages and sunset spots most visitors never find.

Common questions

Before you book

What is included in the homestay?

Your room (AC or non-AC), traditional home-cooked meals, Wi-Fi, safe parking and personal hospitality from our family. We also help with local guidance and transport.

Do you arrange houseboats?

Yes. We arrange private houseboat cruises with a crew, fresh seafood meals and a sunset on the lake. Price depends on duration, season and boat category, so see our price guide or ask for a quote.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out at 11:00 AM. Tell us if you need an early arrival or late departure and we will do our best.

How do I pay?

Cash, UPI or bank transfer. We usually ask for a small deposit to confirm, and we tell you every cost upfront.

When is the best time to visit Kerala?

October to March is the most pleasant for backwater cruises. The monsoon (June to September) is lush and quiet, and April to May is warm but peaceful. Read the full guide.
